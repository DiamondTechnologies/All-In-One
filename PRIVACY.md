# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 4, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how the application ("the App") handles user information.

## 1. Information We Collect

The App does **not** collect, store, or transmit personal information.

In particular, the App does **not** collect:

* Names or email addresses
* IP addresses
* Device identifiers
* Location data
* Usage or behavioral data
* Analytics data
* Advertising identifiers
* Information about your files or their contents

The App does not contain third-party tracking technologies, advertising SDKs, or analytics services.

## 2. Files and Code

The App may access files that you explicitly select or provide for processing.

Any files, source code, or other content processed by the App are handled **locally on your device**. They are not uploaded to our servers or shared with us.

The App does not have a server-side component for collecting or analyzing your files.

You remain responsible for the content of the files you process using the App.

## 3. Third-Party Services

The App does not intentionally integrate third-party services that collect personal information or track users.

If a future version of the App introduces a third-party service that affects user privacy, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

## 4. Data Security

Because the App does not transmit your files or personal information to our servers, your data remains under your control on your device.

However, you are responsible for protecting your device and maintaining appropriate backups of important files.

## 5. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Since the App does not collect personal information, no separate account or age verification is required.

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the App, applicable laws, or our privacy practices.

When making changes, we will make reasonable efforts to notify users of material updates, including by updating this document in the App's official GitHub repository.

The "Last updated" date at the top of this Privacy Policy will be changed whenever the Privacy Policy is updated.

We encourage users to periodically review this Privacy Policy and the official GitHub repository for any changes.

Unless otherwise required by applicable law, changes to this Privacy Policy will become effective when the updated version is published.
